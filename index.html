03

Sources and Standards

The resources draw on professional statements issued by CCCC, CWPA, TYCA, NCTE, MLA, AWAC, and AAC&U, and on published scholarship. Each page lists its sources beside the claims they support.

CCCC statements are cited by title and date of adoption. Links lead to archived copies in the CCCC-L position statement repository (opens in a new tab). See the note on CCCC.

Where a claim rests on professional consensus and not on experimental evidence, the resources say so. Field Commitments explains the distinction.

Key professional issues addressed

  • Teaching load and course caps
  • Contract transparency and salary
  • Curriculum policy under legislative restriction
  • Faculty reappointment and evaluation
  • Teaching modality and scheduling
  • Curricular autonomy and shared governance
04

About the Exchange

Co-directed by Kirsti Cole and Holly Hassel

The Writing Program Exchange began with a collective of writing program practitioners committed to making disciplinary expertise accessible and actionable in institutional contexts. It is a searchable, updatable resource, and not a policy document or a fixed position statement. It adapts to the range of institutions, positions, and challenges WPAs face.

The Exchange does not depend on listserv archives for searching and sorting. It is a stable web environment built to grow over time.

Disciplinary knowledge, organized for institutional action.

A note on CCCC · October 2026

On July 24, 2026, the Executive Committee of the National Council of Teachers of English decided to de-charter the Conference on College Composition and Communication. The Exchange began in a working session at the 2025 CCCC convention, and many of its resources draw on CCCC position statements. Those statements remain the field's record of its professional standards, and the Exchange continues to cite them by title and date of adoption. Archived copies are available in the CCCC-L repository (opens in a new tab). As the transition proceeds, links and citations here will be updated to show where the statements are housed.

Colleagues across the field are doing the work of this transition in different roles, and I am grateful to them. This note is mine alone. It does not speak for my co-director, the Exchange's contributors, or any organization.

Kirsti Cole, Co-Director

Origins

The first resources were drafted by three working groups formed at the 2025 CCCC convention: Policy to Position (labor advocacy), Visualizing Data to Support Change Work, and Crosswalk Reference and Translation.

Contribute

The Writing Program Exchange grows through contributions from practitioners across the field. A WPA with a scenario to share, a researcher with relevant data, and a practitioner who has worked through a specific institutional challenge each strengthen the resource.

Upload materials to the shared Google Drive folder, or write to the co-directors with questions, corrections, and proposals.
